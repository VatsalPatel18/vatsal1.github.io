Project 1 · MedDiscover (Completed Research)

Reproducible biomedical RAG benchmark with two-tier evaluation and reviewer-driven revision

Page 2 of 4 · Paper-First Candidate

Scope and benchmark design

Corpus focuses on metabolomics in metabolic disorders (ICD-10 E70-E88). The revised manuscript reframes MedDiscover as a reproducible benchmark/evaluation pipeline rather than a novel RAG architecture claim.

Gold Set
10 papers / 30 expert QA
Silver Set
100 papers / ~600 synthetic QA
Retriever configs
MedCPT vs Ada-002
Primary decoder
GPT-4o fixed

Method details that strengthen defensibility

500-token chunking with 50 overlap, FAISS top-k retrieval (k=3), evidence-grounded answer prompt, and nonparametric statistics with family-wise correction for the primary RAGAS metric family.

GPT-4.1 decoder runs are presented as sensitivity analysis, not as primary evidence for retriever superiority.

Known limitations (explicitly acknowledged)

Gold sample size remains small; Silver is synthetic silver-standard evidence; 25-token output cap can reduce length-sensitive lexical metrics; broader decoder and real-world cost profiling are future steps.

Key quantitative takeaway

On the 100-paper Silver benchmark, MedCPT and Ada are broadly comparable on retrieval-centric metrics, but MedCPT has a statistically significant gain on answer correctness.

p = 5.72 x 10^-11 Cliff's delta = 0.303 Common-language = 0.651

Professor decision recommendation

Paper-first track. Keep focus on reproducibility, transparent benchmarking, and statistical rigor. Use poster only as communication layer, not as replacement for the manuscript trajectory.

Immediate extensions: larger Gold curation, structured error taxonomy, open biomedical decoder comparison.

Immediate systems question: what quality gain justifies 2B multimodal cost versus stable 1B chat on thermally constrained phones?

Edge deployment thesis

For 6GB phones, the most reliable near-term envelope is 1B-2B decoder-only models in 4-bit weights, with strict control over context/KV cache growth and explicit prefill/decode runtime separation.

4-bit = feasibility anchor Decode = memory-bandwidth bound KV cache = runtime risk center

Poster-to-paper benchmarking plan

Frame AURA as a controlled benchmark study: compare model sizes, quantization levels, and memory policies across device classes using practical metrics.

Devices: 6GB, 8GB, 12GB RAM classes
Metrics: TTFT, decode TPS, peak RAM, battery drain, thermal throttling onset
Reliability: crash/termination rate under long-context and multimodal sessions
Recommendation: demo + poster now, systems paper after benchmark matrix completion.

Project 3 · Abliteration of LLMs (Defensive Research Review)

Safety and governance focus: from single-direction claims toward robust multi-layer defense frameworks

Page 4 of 4 · Review / Position Paper Candidate

Core scientific arc (2024-2026)

Early work highlighted low-dimensional refusal representations in aligned open models. Subsequent work showed richer structure: affine behavior, multiple directions, category-specific geometry, and stronger defense-aware analysis.

2024: refusal-direction hypothesis became central mechanistic lens
2025: methods evolved toward template-robust direction finding and defense training
2026: focus shifts to cross-architecture robustness and evaluation standardization

Responsible framing boundary

This project should be explicitly positioned as defensive and governance-oriented: evaluate robustness, identify brittle assumptions, and propose standards for safer deployment and auditing.

Avoid operational jailbreak replication framing. Emphasize mitigation stacks, threat-model clarity, and policy-ready evaluation protocol.

Why this is high-value now

Institutions and governments need evidence on what alignment can reliably enforce versus what is vulnerable under white-box manipulation in open-weight ecosystems.

Policy relevance Safety evaluation gap Cross-model standards needed

Proposed paper structure

1. Taxonomy and threat-model definitions (white/gray/black-box)
2. Evidence synthesis: where single-direction explanations hold and break
3. Defense stack review: robust training + latent safeguards + evaluation norms
4. Governance implications: red-team protocol and auditability requirements

Professor decision recommendation

Review/position paper is the strongest immediate output. A poster can be used for framing and feedback, but the core value is a rigorous synthesis that is technically precise and policy-aware.

Meeting prompt: proceed as independent review track, or integrate with AURA as a unified safety-evaluation program for edge deployments?
