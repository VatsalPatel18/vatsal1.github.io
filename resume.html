Aug 2024 · Under Journal Review

Immune-Omics Alignment of a Pathology Vision Model

Predicting high-risk HNSCC patients directly from whole-slide images — AUC > 0.85 — by contrastively aligning pathology visual features with immune-omics parameters. Drastically reduces NGS cost and turnaround.

  • Contrastive feature alignment of pathology visuals ↔ immune-omics via cosine-similarity loss.
  • Attention-based MIL head aggregating tile features per WSI (100k × 80k px).
  • Clinical impact: high-risk stratification from an H&E slide alone.
